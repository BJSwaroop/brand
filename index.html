Act III

Don't make videos you think will work.

Make videos people are actually looking for

The strategy

Four steps. Start tonight.

Pick a niche you can defendWhat can you honestly speak on today? Just finished 12th? Make content for students doing 12th.
Fix your audience from day zeroOne clear person you're talking to. Every video.
Study 10 channels like yoursNote what worked. Shortlist 1–2 concepts from each that fit you. Now you have 10–20 proven ideas.
One video a weekFrom that shortlist. Don't reinvent. Iterate.

RememberOne video a week beats ten videos and then silence.
